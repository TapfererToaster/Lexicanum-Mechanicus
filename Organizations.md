# Internet Assigned Numbers Authority (IANA)
The *IANA* manages both IPv4 and IPv6 addresses and allocates blocks of them to the *Regional Internet Registries (RIR)*. 
## Regional Internet Registries (RIR)
RIRs are responsible for allocating IP addresses to ISPs, who provide address blocks to organizations and smaller ISPs.

# Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE)
This organization defined the [[Ethernet|802.3 (Ethernet)]], in 1983, and [[WLAN#IEEE 802.11 standards|802.11 (Wi-Fi)]] standards.
## Groups
**Working groups**
Are assigned to develop specific technologies, with the two main ones being 802.3 and 802.11

**Task groups**
Task group are assigned inside each working group to revise and continue the development of the original standards. 
Each task group is assigned a letter in alphabetical order (f.e. 802.3a, 802.3b, ...). Once all letters are used another letter is added and are also incremented in alphabetical order (f.e. 802.3aa-802.3az, 802.3ba-802.3.bz, ...).
