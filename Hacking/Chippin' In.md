# When stuck
**Take a break**
"There is more to life than SQL injections and XSS payloads."
When you do not find bugs or vulnerabilities you should take a break and rest or do something else.

**Learn new Skills**
When stuck and or in a slump, you can use it as an opportunity to improve your skills or learn something new.
For example:
- learn a new technique or exploit; [OWASP](https://owasp.org/) 
- test a different platform; new OS, or application
- play CTFs

**Return to your target**
When you feel ready again, return to your target. Define a specific target and test new skills you learned.
It can also help when there are multiple targets and you switch between them when you get fatigued.
